1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Local disaster-risk offices (CDRRMO/MDRRMO), environmental researchers, and planners in the Philippines frequently consult near-real-time satellite hotspot maps, but raw maps alone cannot tell them whether current activity is genuinely unusual for a given province and season. FIRE MEMORY closes three critical analytical gaps:

  • Cross-Sensor Discontinuity: Legacy MODIS (1 km) and newer VIIRS (375 m) instruments differ in spatial resolution, detection sensitivity, and pass schedules. Comparing raw counts directly mistakes a sensor transition for a real change in thermal activity.
  • Missing Seasonal Context: Comparing November against an all-year average confuses dry-season peaks with rainy-season baselines. FIRE MEMORY compares each selected UTC month strictly against the same calendar month's historical median.
  • Hidden Uncertainty & Zero-Bias: Incomplete data is often rendered as zero, hiding coverage gaps. FIRE MEMORY distinguishes No data from 0, separates observed VIIRS rows from estimated MODIS conversions, and reports calibration error transparently.

2. Data Provenance & MODIS-to-VIIRS Harmonization

The current audited workspace ingestion processes 12 local NASA FIRMS downloads covering 2020-01-01 through 2025-12-31 across Philippines geoBoundaries ADM0 and ADM2 shapes:

VIIRS NOAA-20 (N20, v2) 6 yearly CSVs 153,533 Primary calibration target scale (VIIRS-equivalent) using 0.01° UTC cell-days.
Total Audited Local Archive 12 files 177,843 Custom drawn bounding boxes aggregate against coarser 0.1° grid cells (grid-approximated).

Calibration Method (median-monthly-viirs-over-modis-v1)

  • Uses only province/month pairs where both MODIS and VIIRS recorded positive detections (2,539 pairs across 72 national months in candidate overlap years 2020–2025).
  • Province factor = median of monthly VIIRS / MODIS active-cell-day ratios (minimum 12 matched months spanning ≥ 3 years); 65 provinces use local factors and 16 use the national fallback.
  • Provisional national factor: 4.09777677.

Measured Validation & Limitations

  • In-sample national correlation: 0.9928 (MAPE 28.68%).
  • Leave-one-year-out (LOYO) pooled correlation: 0.9922 (MAPE 29.20%, monthly anomaly agreement 91.67% across 72 months).
  • Provisional Caution: Provincial MAPE varies (median 88.93%); missing archive retrieval manifests mean figures remain labeled provisional rather than asserting instrument equivalence.

4. User Guide for CDRRMO Officers & Analysts

4.1 Map Workspace (/)

  1. Inspect Top Summary Cards: View live/monthly totals across five frosted cards: Total Fire Hotspots, Small-Area Sensor (VIIRS 375m), Wide-Area Sensor (MODIS 1km), Typical Baseline (Median), and Comparison vs. Typical Month.
  2. Open Map Filters (Pin Button, top-right): Choose a province/region, set UTC month (YYYY-MM), toggle live windows (Today, 7 days, Month, Year), switch between Harmonized and Raw sensor metrics, or draw a custom bounding-box rectangle directly on the map.
  3. Examine Clusters & Intensity Legend: Click cluster markers or province bubbles to zoom in; refer to the bottom-left Hotspot Intensity Scale (Low, Moderate, High, Very High).

4.2 CDRRMO Advisory AI & Priority Watchlist (Advisory AI Button)

  1. Click Advisory AI on the right side of the map to open the CDRRMO Fire Advisory drawer.
  2. Review the live VIIRS and MODIS breakdown strip, then click Get CDRRMO Advisory to generate grounded Summary, CDRRMO Action, and Note cards tailored to BFP & Barangay DRRMC verification.
  3. Expand Ask a specific question (optional) to choose a prompt or type your own data question; the AI rejects invented numbers, spread forecasts, and evacuation directives.
  4. Switch to the Watchlist tab to jump directly to high-activity provinces nationally or inspect Bukidnon's Northern, Central (Malaybalay–Valencia), and Southern (Maramag–Quezon) monitoring corridors.

4.3 Historical Calendar (/calendar) & Anomaly Detection (/anomaly)

  • Activity Calendar: Displays every year-by-month detection count side by side; distinct patterns flag Estimated conversions and muted No data gaps without zero-filling. Clicking any cell selects that period globally. Supports Dark and Warm display modes.
  • Anomaly Detection: Renders a same-month year-by-year bar chart with the dashed Typical Median Line, visual baseline meter, CDRRMO operational posture badge (ROUTINE MONITORING through PRIORITY GROUND VERIFICATION), the MODIS Raw / Scaled / VIIRS Overlap Chart, and the Harmonization Panel.

4.4 CSV Export (/export)

  • Hotspot Coordinates CSV: Exports up to 10,000 coordinate rows (id, latitude, longitude, acquisitionDatetime, satellite, instrument, confidence, brightness, sourceProduct).
  • Monthly Baseline & Anomaly CSV: Exports multi-year monthly counts paired with baseline median, anomaly percentage, and classification label for offline LGU briefings.
